2. Website hosting and access data

This static website is published using GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you access the website, technical information needed to deliver the pages may be transmitted to and processed by the hosting provider, including your IP address, requested page, and connection-related data. GitHub expressly states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub.

The purpose is secure, reliable delivery of the website and protection against misuse. Where this processing is attributable to us as the website operator, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For GitHub's own processing purposes and practices, please consult GitHub's Privacy Statement.

GitHub is a US-based provider. Processing and access outside the European Economic Area cannot be ruled out. For applicable international transfer information and safeguards, refer to GitHub's privacy documentation. We do not determine GitHub's independently controlled security-log retention periods; further details are provided by GitHub.

3. Theme preference and browser storage

Our website offers Light and Dark modes. It initially follows your device's color preference. Only if you actively switch the theme do we store the selected value in your browser's localStorage under the key d2ce4i-theme. This allows the website to remember your expressly requested display preference on subsequent visits. The stored value is either light or dark; it is not transmitted to us by the theme-switching code.

You can clear this preference through your browser's site-data controls. We use this storage solely to provide the display setting you requested, not for analytics or advertising. Its legal assessment is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR where personal data are involved and the exception for strictly necessary storage for a user-requested service under Section 25(2) TDDDG.

4. Contact by email

When you choose to email us, we process the information contained in your message, such as your email address, name (if provided), message content and related communication metadata, so that we can handle your enquiry and reply. Depending on the enquiry, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (responding to general enquiries) or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps requested before entering into a contract).

Providing information by email is voluntary; without a return address or sufficient context we may not be able to respond. Messages are kept only as long as necessary for the enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations or legitimate legal-claim purposes require longer storage. Email transmission also involves the mail services used by the sender and recipient.

5. Cookies, analytics and external content

The website's own HTML and JavaScript do not include advertising trackers, analytics services, embedded third-party media, contact forms or marketing cookies. The theme preference described above is the only browser storage intentionally used by our website code. This does not describe or exclude technical processing independently performed by GitHub Pages or your browser.

6. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may request access to your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability where applicable (Art. 20). You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

The supervisory authority potentially responsible for our private-sector processing in Bavaria is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA). You may also contact another competent supervisory authority.

7. Automated decisions and updates

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR on this concept website. We may update this notice if the website, the services we use or legal requirements change.
